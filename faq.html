Frequently Asked Questions

How can we help?

Let us help you with your investment concerns. Below are some of the most common questions we receive about Gold and Silver IRAs.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we can ship precious metals internationally. Please contact us for more details about shipping options and costs.

What are the prices of the precious metals that you offer?

The prices of precious metals fluctuate based on market conditions. Contact us for the latest pricing or visit our website for live updates.

Can I pay cash and get metals delivered to my home?

Yes, you can purchase precious metals outright and have them delivered to your home. Contact us to learn more about this option.

What if I want to take delivery or cash out of my precious metals?

You can take delivery or liquidate your precious metals at any time. Contact us to discuss the process and any associated fees.

Are my physical Gold and Silver safe in the depository?

Yes, your precious metals are stored in secure, insured depositories. You can rest assured that your investments are safe.

Do I own physical precious metals with a Gold or Silver IRA?

Yes, with a Gold or Silver IRA, you own physical precious metals that are stored in a secure depository.

Are there any taxes or penalties to move the funds?

Moving funds into a Gold or Silver IRA is typically a tax-free process. Consult with your tax advisor for specific details.

How do the funds get moved? Do I have to do anything?

We handle the transfer process for you. Our team will work with your current custodian to ensure a smooth transition.
